FROSTFALL

Keep the last fire alive

The world froze over. One furnace is all that stands between your camp and the endless cold.

  • 🪓 Walk into trees to chop wood. Logs stack on your back.
  • 🔥 Carry them to the furnace. A bigger fire means a wider warm zone.
  • ❄️ Stand beside frozen survivors to thaw them out.
  • 🏕️ Drop logs on build pads to grow your camp.
  • 🧭 Build a Scout Post for daily expeditions: pick the best number gates to grow your group, then defeat the wolf pack for rewards.
  • 🐺 Wolves hunt at night, and a giant Ice Bear comes on nights 3 and 5. Survive 5 nights, or build the Rescue Beacon to escape early!

Move: WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on touch screens
